# Mathematics - Large Numbers Around Us

## One Lakh

One lakh means 100,000. It is written as 1,00,000 in the Indian number system.

Students can understand one lakh by thinking about repeated counting. If someone tries one new rice variety every day, then in 100 years they would try about 36,500 varieties, which is still less than one lakh.

## Place Value

Place value helps us understand large numbers. In 1,00,000, the digit 1 is in the lakh place.
